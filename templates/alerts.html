About Alerts

Alerts are generated when incoming logs match your configured filters. Each alert shows:

  • Time: When the alert was triggered
  • Severity: The log severity level
  • Filter: Which filter was matched
  • Source: The log source (server, container, etc.)
  • Message: The log message that triggered the alert

Acknowledge alerts to mark them as reviewed. If Telegram notifications are enabled, alerts will be sent to your configured chat.
